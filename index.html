Expenses

Record costs you pay for the business. Enter only the business share of anything you also use privately. Client entertaining is not an allowable expense. Categories follow the Self Assessment self-employment pages (SA103S short form boxes 11 to 19, with the SA103F full form box in brackets).

Billfold is an invoice and expense worksheet, not accounting software. It does not send invoices, does not file VAT or tax returns, and is not tax advice. Check your figures before you send them.

Billfold is not Making Tax Digital (MTD) software. It cannot keep MTD digital records for HMRC or send updates or returns. If MTD for Income Tax applies to you (from 6 April 2026 if your qualifying income was over £50,000 in 2024 to 2025; the threshold falls to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028), you need HMRC-recognised software. VAT-registered businesses must use MTD-compatible software for VAT returns.

Sources: GOV.UK "Invoices: what they must include"; HMRC VAT Notice 700 (sections 14, 16 and 17.5); SA103S and SA103F 2025 to 2026; GOV.UK "Find out if and when you need to use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax". Checked 4 October 2026.
